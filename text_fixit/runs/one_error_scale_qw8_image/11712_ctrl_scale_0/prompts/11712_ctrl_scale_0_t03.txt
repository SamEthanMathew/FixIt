FAULT SCALE
Faults are not subtle. A displaced part is typically moved 0.080-0.200 m; a tilted part is
rotated 15-45 degrees; a mis-sized part is scaled by a factor of 1.25-1.60. Your repair has to
undo a fault of that size, so it must be of the SAME MAGNITUDE in the opposite direction - a
translation or rotation of the same size with the opposite sign, or for size, the RECIPROCAL of the
factor (a part enlarged 1.4x is repaired by scaling it 1/1.4 = 0.71).
A few millimetres, or a few degrees, will not fix anything. If a repair leaves the part still
visibly wrong, your magnitude was too SMALL, not too large - increase it.

FRAME AND UNITS
Right-handed object frame; the axes X, Y, Z are the coordinate axes in the part table (centres and
bounding boxes). Lengths in metres, rotations in degrees (right-hand rule).
  TRANSLATE moves the whole part along an axis.
  ROTATE turns the part in place about its own CENTROID, around the given axis.
  SCALE resizes the part along an axis about its hinge-side edge.

ACTION SPACE
Emit exactly one action:
  TRANSLATE(part, axis, value)   axis in {X,Y,Z}, value in metres,    any value in [-0.4, 0.4] m (continuous)

  SCALE(part, axis, value)       axis in {X,Y,Z}, value a multiplier, any multiplier in [0.549, 1.822] (continuous)
  NO_FIX()                       the object is already functional
Only parts marked fixable=yes may be targeted.

A repair is a single (type, part, axis, value) tuple - you may not compose two actions.

TOLERANCE

closes, and no parts interpenetrate.
